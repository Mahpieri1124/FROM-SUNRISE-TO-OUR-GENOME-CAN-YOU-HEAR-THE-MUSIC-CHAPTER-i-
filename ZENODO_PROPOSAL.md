## Proposed Abstract for Zenodo

This research proposal develops an interdisciplinary empirical programme
investigating potential relationships among environmental acoustic
structure, natural soundscapes, human musicality, auditory prediction,
neural dynamics, physiological regulation, gene expression and
gene--environment interaction. The project is motivated by the question
"From Sunrise to Our Genome --- Can You Hear the Music?" and seeks to
convert a broad philosophical and interdisciplinary intuition into a
falsifiable research architecture.

The proposal does not claim that music is literally encoded in the human
genome or that musical exposure changes DNA sequence. Instead, it
distinguishes genetic variation, gene expression, epigenetic regulation,
neuroplasticity, physiology and cultural learning as separate levels of
analysis.

Existing research provides evidence for biological components of
musicality, genetic contributions to musical traits, shared genetic
architecture between rhythm and language-related traits, beneficial
effects of some natural sound exposures, and molecular/neuroplastic
changes associated with music engagement. However, these literatures
remain fragmented. The proposed programme therefore focuses on the
integration and causal testing of the links between acoustic
environment, musical cognition, neural prediction, physiological
regulation and molecular response.

The programme is hypothesis-generating and explicitly open to
falsification. Its first practical objective is operationalisation and
controlled experimental testing, followed by replication, naturalistic
field research and only then possible applications in health, wellbeing,
nature-based tourism and social cohesion.
